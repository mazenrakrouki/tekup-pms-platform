\chapter*{\Huge Dedication}
\vspace{8mm}

{\itshape

Al hamdulillah. Before anyone else, I thank God, for every door that opened and for every one
that stayed closed, for the patience given to me when I had none left of my own, and for the path
He chose for me. None of what follows would exist without Him.

To my mother, Afifa, who believed in me long before I had done anything to deserve it. Not only
through this year, but through every year before it, through every exam, every doubt and every
beginning again. Your support never once depended on a result. You carried this road with me, and
you carried more of it than I did.

To my father, Tawfik, for the advice I did not always want to hear and always ended up needing,
and for a support that has never announced itself and has never been absent. Not this year alone,
but across the whole of my studies and my life. Whatever I have built stands on ground you laid.

To my brothers, Bessem and Jesser, and to my little sister Kinza: you kept me patient when my own
patience had run out, you pushed me to continue when stopping would have been easier, and you have
been the support standing quietly behind me the whole way. You are the reason a demanding year
still had good days in it.

To Roua, my love, who lived through the long evenings and the weeks when this work took everything
I had, and who never made me feel the cost of it. Your patience is in these pages, even if your
name appears only on this one.

To my close friends, Selim, Ghassen and Amine, and to my cousin Achref, for the company, for the
laughter, and for reminding me that there was a life on the other side of the screen.

To everyone who, with a word or a gesture, helped me reach the end of this road.

\vspace{7mm}
\begin{flushright}
May God keep you all, and grant you the health and the happiness you have wished for me.
\end{flushright}

\vspace{9mm}
\begin{flushright}
{\Large Mazen Rakrouki}
\end{flushright}

}
